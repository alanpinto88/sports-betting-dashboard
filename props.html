How the signals work

Line gap: sportsbook line minus PrizePicks projection. A positive number is not automatically an edge: prices, payouts, and market rules differ.

Rebound watch: recent average below the PrizePicks projection and the newest two games below the first three. This flags a question for further research, not evidence of a bounce-back.

Goblin / Demon: special difficulty and payout modifiers. Evaluate the actual side and multiplier, not the color alone. Alternate lines cannot be compared as though they have identical payouts.

Five-game hit rate is descriptive only. No model-derived probability, expected value, or automatic recommendation is published without validated historical data and payout information.
