Discover matching player props and compare their lines. A difference is a research lead, not proven +EV. EXTREME is a numerical severity label, not a betting recommendation. No wagering, purchases, or automatic bet recommendations.

Data connection

Demo mode uses fictional sample data and is not live. A live feed requires a server-side connection with an authorized API key; never paste a secret into this public website. Source: independent PrizePicksAPI.com comparison feed, not affiliated with PrizePicks. Free-plan comparisons can be delayed 5–10 minutes. Supported sportsbook coverage is limited.

Major-gap thresholds (editable in a future release): NFL receiving/rushing yards ≥25 AND ≥30% of the lower line; passing yards ≥60 AND ≥25% of the lower line; receptions ≥3; NBA points ≥5, rebounds ≥3, assists ≥3, threes ≥2; NHL shots on goal ≥2, saves ≥5. For NFL yardage, both the absolute and relative requirements must be met. Other markets are not flagged until a market-specific threshold is defined. The optional buffer increases these thresholds. Extreme gaps require twice the normal absolute threshold and a larger relative difference for NFL yardage. These are screening rules, not statistically validated edges.
