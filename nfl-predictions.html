Research method

Use pregame odds snapshots, source-labeled ticket and handle splits, injury and lineup reports, weather, rest, home/away splits, and independently verified final scores. Evaluate separately by market and kickoff slot; track pushes, prices, missing data, confidence intervals and return per unit staked. Compare minority-ticket selections with a no-signal baseline. Reject stale or contradictory inputs and avoid claiming that a line move proves sharp money.
